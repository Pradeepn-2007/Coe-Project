## Test steps

1. Ask the student to add at least three realistic academic tasks with different deadlines, priorities and effort estimates.
2. Before showing the Smart Priority recommendation, ask: **“Which task would you work on first, and why?”**
3. Record the selected task and the student's reason below.
4. Show the Smart Priority recommendation and the “Why this task?” score breakdown.
5. Ask the student to explain whether the recommendation makes sense. Do not lead the answer.
6. Ask the student to rate usefulness and note any confusion or mismatch.
